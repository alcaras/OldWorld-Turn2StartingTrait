Turn 2 Starting Trait — an Old World mod
========================================

WHAT IT DOES
  With Leader set to "Pick Later" and Customize Leader OFF, your leader no longer
  rolls a random strength trait when you found your capital. Instead, on turn 2 you
  get the usual "Promote" popup and choose the trait yourself: four traits, no
  rating options.

  If the leader already holds a job (general / explorer / governor) the popup keeps
  the stock level-up shape: three traits for that job plus one from the other pool.

  Your archetype pick and your ratings are untouched. Players using a fixed
  archetype, a preset leader, or the leader customizer are unaffected.

INSTALL
  Copy the "OwTraitMod" folder into your Old World Mods directory:

    macOS    ~/Library/Application Support/OldWorld/Mods/
    Windows  %USERPROFILE%\Documents\My Games\OldWorld\Mods\
    Linux    ~/.local/share/OldWorld/Mods/

  Then launch the game, open Mods, enable "Turn 2 Starting Trait", and restart when
  prompted. Start a New Game with Leader = "Pick Later" and Customize Leader off.

VERIFY
  Turn 1, after founding: your leader's panel shows the archetype and no strength
  trait. Turn 2: the "Promote <leader>" popup appears with four traits.

  The mod also writes to the game log, if you want to be sure:
    macOS    ~/Library/Application Support/OldWorld/Logs/output.txt
    Windows  %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Mohawk Games\Old World\output_log.txt
  Look for lines beginning "[OwTraitMod]".

NOTES
  Built against Old World 1.0.84365. Single player and multiplayer; every player in
  the game needs the mod enabled for a multiplayer match.
